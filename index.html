Our Process

Sketch the crossing

We map the landmarks, name the decision the player must make, and sketch what the coast looks like at both tides.

Build a playable room

One designer and one engineer build the smallest room that lets a player try that decision.

Watch someone play

We watch where players pause, change one thing, and test the crossing again.
